Cypherpunk values.

Yes, a cypherpunk dinner. In Washington.

Crypto’s founding ideas deserve a place in the policy conversation: privacy as a right, control over your own money, and financial systems open to everyone.

PGPZ is convening the builders, advocates, and policy leaders who can move that conversation forward. Project Glitch brings its programming and cypherpunk perspective to the evening. The ambition: make financial privacy a starting point for policy, rather than an afterthought.

If you build it, defend it, or want policy to protect it, you belong at this table.
